#### Intro
The "MyDrive" platform is a comprehensive vehicle assistance and garage services management solution designed to connect customers with various service providers, including roadside assistance , towing services, garage services and spare parts purchase. The platform aims to streamline the coordination and delivery of automotive services through a centralized, digital environment.

Main focused areas,
- Roadside assistance
- Towing services
- Garage services
- Spare parts selling

#### Quantitative Landscape of Sri Lankan Automotive Market
The viability of any on-demand service platform similar to "MyDrive" is fundamentally depend on the size and health of the vehicle population. 
For MyDrive, the total addressable market(TAM) includes every registered motor vehicle in Sri Lanka as,
- Each vehicle requires periodic maintenance
- Emergency situations or
- Component replacements

**Market Trends and Customer Sentiments** 
Trajectory of the Sri Lankan vehicle market is set by,
- The high cost of new vehicles
- Previous import restrictions
- Currency depreciation
These reasons forced many owners to retain the older vehicles for longer periods than previously intended.  This trend has led to a consistent rise in the frequency of repairs and a booming market for aftermarket spare parts and accessories. 

**Strategic Drivers and Market Opportunities**
The rise of the Sri Lankan EV vehicle segment is a primary driver for MyDrive. This market is projected to grow from a valuation of $12.5 billion in 2025 to over $19.3 billion by 2031, representing a compound annual growth rate(CAGR) of 7.8%. 
https://www.dhl.com/discover/en-lk/b2b-advice/b2b-e-commerce-resources/future-of-sri-lanka-automotive-industry

The adoption of EVs represents unique logistic challenges, particularly regarding high-voltage battery handling and specialized diagnostics. Current unorganized garages are largely ill-quipped to handle these requirements, creating a massive opportunity for a platform like MyDrive to certify and onboard specialized EV service providers. 

Modern vehicle owners are increasingly seeking predictive maintenance and real-time monitoring with AI and IoT integrations. MyDrive's integration of AI-driven service recommendations based on user history and location aligns perfectly with these global trends, allowing the platform to move from a reactive emergency services to a proactive vehicle management tool. (MyDrive SRS)

**Chronic Pain Points in the Service Ecosystem**
The following table highlights the primary pain points that MyDrive seeks to resolve, 

| Pain point         | Current market reality                                                      | MyDrive solution                                                             |
| ------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Price transparency | Opaque pricing. Arbitrary charges by roadside mechanics during emergencies. | Standardized pricing menus and pre-approved service pakcages.                |
| Response delays    | No reliable way to track. Depend on phone calls and verbal estimates.       | Real-time GPS tracking of service providers and automate ETAs.               |
| Part authenticity  | High prevalence of counterfeit or substandard components.                   | Multi-vendor marketplace with verified sellers and genuine brand warranties. |
| Quality trust      | Reliance on unverified word-of-mouth or social media recommendations.       | Private rating systems and review moderation to ensure provider credibility. |
MyDrive's role in verifying the credentials of its vendors servers not only as a business differentiator but as a critical public safety function since counterfeit parts(brake shoes, engine part, bearings,...) have been identified as major contributor to road accidents in Sri Lanka.
https://www.customs.gov.lk/destruction-of-counterfeit-and-pirated-goods/
