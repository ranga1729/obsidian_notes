## Model Re-usability into other industries
A job offering site.

1. Residential maintenance and the On-demand home service marketplace. 
	Homeowners increasingly demand the same level of transparency and real-time coordination for plumbing, electrical, and HVAC services. The "Needs-Based" nature of a car breakdown on a highway is functionally equivalent to a burst pipe in a kitchen; both require immediate dispatch, verified skills, and transparent pricing.
	https://www.fatbit.com/fab/build-on-demand-service-marketplace-platform/

- The `Job Service` would be modified to handle "Service Categories" such as "Plumbing Emergency," "Electrical Troubleshooting," or "Routine HVAC Tuning". 
- The "Roadside Assistance" role from the MyDrive SRS is repurposed into "Home Service Professionals". 
- The `Spare Parts Marketplace` in MyDrive, originally for tires and engine components, is customized into a "Home Hardware Store" where users can purchase faucets, circuit breakers, or filters recommended by their technician. Or we can turn the spare parts market place into a unified market place for hardware shops.
Ex: https://ciiclo.ca/10-best-on-demand-home-service-marketplaces-2025/
