International Convention on Cybercrime would include,
- Illegal access to computers
- Illegal interception of data in transit or on storage including denial of access to data.
- Data inference
- System inference
- Misuse of devices
- Computer-related forgery
- Computer-related fraud
- Offences related to child pornography

- Cybercrimes require proper investigation with sophisticated grasp of the technology.

#### Intellectual Property
Types of properties,
1. Real properties: Lands and things permanently attached to the land. 
2. Personal property: Movable property and goods
	ex: cars, bank accounts, wages, small business, jewelry, insurance policies, patents, pets
3. Intellectual property: Any intangible asset that consists of human knowledge and ideas.
	ex: software, data, novels, sound recordings, designs, cure for a design

Types of intellectual property which legal protection is available,
1. Copyright
2. Trademarks
3. Patents

- "Infringement" is the legal protection against the invasion of the rights secured by copyright, trademarks and patents. 
- The right to seek civil recourse against anyone infringing his or her property is granted to the IP owner.
	- copyright - unauthorized use
	- trademarks - unauthorized use or colorable imitation
	- patents - unauthorized making, using, or selling
- Copyright law protects the tangible or fixed expression of an idea, not the idea itself. 
- A creator can claim copyright, and file for the copyright at a national government copyright office, if the following conditions are fulfilled,
	- The proposed work is original
	- The creator has put this original idea into a concreate form
- Copyright owners rights, 
	1. Reproduction right
		The owner can make copies of a work
	2. Modification right: 
		AKA "derivative-works" right. 
		the owner can modify a work to create a new or derivative work
	3. Distribution right
		The owner can publicly sell, rent, lease, or lend copies of the work
	4. Public-performance right
	5. Public-display right
		The owner can publicly show a copy of the work directly or by means of a film, slide, or television image
- A patent = The grant of a property rights to the inventor

- Types of patents,
	1. Utility patents
		Granted to anyone who invents or discovers any new and useful process, machine, article of manufacture, or composition of matter, or any new and useful improvement.
	2. Design patents
		Granted to anyone who invents a new, original, and ornamental design for an article of manufacture.
	3. Plant patents
		Granted to anyone who invents or discovers and asexually reproduces any distinct and new variety of plant.

- Intellectual property that are relevant to computers,
	1. Software
		Includes programs produced by vendors of commercial software as well as shareware, proprietary software created by an organization for internal use, and software produced by individuals.
	2. Databases
		data that is collected and organized in such a fashion that it has potential commercial value
	3. Digital content
		audio, video, multimedia, courseware, web content, ...
	4. Algorithm
		Ex: RSA public-key cryptosystem is patentable

- Digital Rights Management(DRM) refers to systems and procedures that ensure that holders of digital rights are clearly identified and receive the stipulated payment for their works.
- User types of DRMs,
	1. Content provider
	2. Distributor
	3. Consumer
	4. Clearinghouse

#### Privacy
Areas of privacy,
1. Anonymity
	Use a resource or service without disclosing the user's identity. 
2. Pseudonymity
	Use a resource or service without disclosing its user identity, but can still be accountable for that use. 
3. Unlinkability
	User may make multiple uses of resources or services without others being able to link these uses together
4. Unobservability
	user may use a resource or service without others being able to observe that the resource or service is being used.

#### Sri Lanka computer Crime Act
- Act = No.24 of 2007
- It identifies several actions committed by people, or organizations as punishable offenses in accordance with the provisions of the Code of Criminal Procedure Act, No. 15 of 1979.
- The law is applicable irrespective of whether the considered crime is conducted in Sri Lanka or outside Sri Lanka.

Types of crimes identified by the act,
1. Unauthorized access to a computer
2. Doing any unauthorized access in order to commit an offence
3. Causing a computer to perform a function without lawful authority
4. Offences committed against national security
5. Dealing with data that are unlawfully obtained
6. Illegal interception of data
7. Use of illegal devices
8. Unauthorized disclosure of information enabling access to a service

- To facilitate investigation on computer crimes, police officers can assign experts to the case. 
	Such experts are basically appointed from universities that are established through the Universities Act, No. 16 of 1978.
- Once appointed, such experts can,
	- enter upon any premises along with a police officer not below the rank of a sub-inspector.
	- access any information system, computer or computer system or any programme, data or information held in such computer to perform any function or to do any such other thing
	- require any person to disclose any traffic data
	- orally examine any person
	- do such other things as may be reasonably required
- Any person who is required to make any disclosure or to assist in an investigation under this Act, shall comply with such requirement.
- A person who obstructs the lawful exercise of the powers conferred on an expert or a police officer or fails to comply with such request made by such expert or police officer during an investigation shall be guilty of an offence and shall on conviction be liable to a fine or imprisonment.
	Ex: When a computer is locked, the owner is obliged to surrender the password to the investigator.
