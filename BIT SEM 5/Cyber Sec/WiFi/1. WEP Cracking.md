WEP stands for "Wired Equivalent Privacy". 
#### What are initialization Vectors(IVs) ?
In encryption, and IV is a small random looking value added to the encryption process to make sure the same plaintext doesn't always result in the same ciphertext. 
In WEP, the IV is 24 bits long and is sent in plaintext alongside each encrypted packet.
