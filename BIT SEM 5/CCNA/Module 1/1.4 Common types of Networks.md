- The internet is a collection of interconnected private and public networks("network of networks").
- Types of networks based on the size,
	1. Small home networks
	2. Small office and home office networks(SOHO)
	3. Medium to large networks
	4. World Wide networks
#### LANs and WANs
- Network infrastructures vary on,
	1. Size of the area
	2. Number of users
	3. Number and types of services
	4. Area of responsibility
- LAN : Network infrastructure that provides access to users and end devices in a small geographical area.
	Ex: Department of an enterprise, a home, small business network
- WAN : Network infrastructure that provides access to other networks over a wide geographical area.
	Typically managed by a large corporation or a telco service provider.

#### LANs
- Spans a small geo area.
- Characteristics,
	1. Interconnect end devices in a limited area
	2. Administered by a single org or an individual
	3. Provide high-speed bandwidth to internal devices.
![[Pasted image 20251208200537.png]]
#### WANs
![[Pasted image 20251208201637.png]]
- WAN connects multiple LANs. 
- WANs are typically managed by service providers(SPs) or Internet Service Provider(ISPs).
- Characteristics,
	1. Interconnect LANs over a wide geo area. 
	2. Administered by multiple service providers.
	3. Typically provide slower speed links between LANs. 
#### The Internet
![[Pasted image 20251208202218.png]]
- Internet can be seen as a collection of interconnected LANs and WANs. 
- Some LANs connected through a WAN connection. 
- WANs are then connected to each other. 
- Internet is not owned by an individual or group. 
- There are some organizations that were developed to help maintain the structure and standardization of internet protocols and processes. 
	Ex: 
	Internet Engineering Task Force(IETF)
	Internet Corporation for Assigned Names and Numbers(ICANN)
	Internet Architecture Board(IAB)
#### Intranets and Extranets
- Intranet : Private connection of LANs and WANs that belongs to an organization. Designed to be accessible by the organization's members, employees or other with authorization. 
- Extranet : Provide secure access to individuals who work for a different organization but require access to the organization's data.
	Ex:
	A company provide access to outside suppliers and contractors.
	A hospital provide a booking system for patients.
