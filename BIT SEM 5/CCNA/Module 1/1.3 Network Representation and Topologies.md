These diagrams are called "topology diagrams."

Terminology,
1. Network Interface Card(NIC)
	Physically connects end devices to the network. 
2. Physical Port
	A connector/outlet on a networking device where media connects to an end device or another networking device.
3. Interface
	Specialized ports on a networking device that connect to individual networks. 
	Ex: Routers connect networks, since then, ports on a router are referred to as 'network interfaces'.
#### Topology Diagrams
Two types,
1. Physical 
	Illustrate the physical location of intermediary devices and cable installation. 
	Ex: Devices labeled with their physical locations(room no, server, rack, shelf)
	![[Pasted image 20251208152353.png]]
2. Logical
	Illustrate devices, ports and the addressing scheme of the network. 
	Ex: Labeling shows which device is connected to which intermediary device and what medium is used for the connection.
