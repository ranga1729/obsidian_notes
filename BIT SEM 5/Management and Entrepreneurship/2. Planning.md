Planning involves determining the organization's goals and defining the means of achieving them. 
A goal : a desired future state that the organization attempts to realize.
A plan: a blueprint for goal achievement and specifies the necessary resource allocations, schedules, tasks and other actions.

#### 1. Develop the plan
**Define Mission**
	Mission: The organization's reason for existence.
	It describes the organization's values, aspirations and reason for being exist. 
	A well defined mission is the basis for development of all subsequent goals and plans. 
Ex: Unilever
	Our mission is to add Vitality to life. We meet everyday needs for nutrition, hygiene and personal care with brands that help people look good, feel good and get more out of life

**Set Goals**
- Strategic goals/official goals: Broad statements describing where the organization wants to be in the future. Pertain to the organization as a whole. 
- Tactical goals: Results that major division and departments within the organization intended to achieve. Apply to middle management and describe what major subunits must do for the organization to achieve its overall goals. 
- Operational goals: Result expected from departments, work groups and individuals. Precise and measurable. 
#### 2. Translate the plan
**Define Tactical Plans**
	Tactical plans are designed to help execute the major strategic plans and to accomplish a specific part of the company's strategy. 
**Develop Strategy Map**
	Strategy map is a visual representation of the key drivers(learning and growth, internal processes, customer services, financial performance) of an organizations's success. 
**Define Contingency Plans**
	Contingency plan is a company response in emergencies, setbacks or unexpected conditions. 
	Managers has to identify important factors such as possible economic downturns, declining markets, increase in cost of supplies, new technological developments or safety accidents. 

#### 3. Plan Operations
**Define Operational Plans**
	Operational plans specify action steps towards achieving operational goals and to support tactical plans. 
	Developed at the lower level of the organization.
	Schedules are an important component of an operational plan.
**Define Measures and Targets**
	Goals need to be specific and measurable. (Some goals can not be expressed in numerical terms.)
	Then define KPIs.
#### 4. Execute the Plan
**Management By Objectives(MBO)**
	MBO is system whereby managers and employees define goals for every department, project, and person and use them to monitor subsequent performance.

**Single use plans** are developed to achieve a set of goals that are not likely to be repeated in the future. 
	Ex: Programs and projects
**Standing plans** are ongoing plans that provide guidance for tasks or situations that occur repeatedly within the organization. 
	Ex: Policies, rules, procedures

#### Benefits of Planning
Goals and plans
 - Provide a source of motivation and commitment. 
- Guide resource allocation.
- Guide to actions.
- Set a standard of performance. 
#### Limitations
Goals and plans can create,
- Can create a false sense of certainty. 
- May cause rigidity in a turbulent environment
- Can get in the way of intuition and creativity
