#### Managerial Skills
1. Conceptual Skills : Cognitive ability to see the organization as a whole and the relationship among its part. 
2. Human Skills : Ability to work well with other.
3. Technical Skills : Ability of understanding and performing specific/operational tasks.

#### Types of Managers
This is the vertical hierarchy of managers.
![[Pasted image 20250831155459.png]]

Horizontal comparison of managers are,
1. Functional Managers : Responsible for departments that perform a single functional task and have employees with similar training and skills.
	Ex: Advertising, Sales, Finance, HR, Manufacturing, Accounting
2. Line Managers : Responsible for the manufacturing and marketing departments that make or sell the products or services.
3. Staff Managers : In-charge of departments such as Finance and Human Resources that support line departments
4. General Managers : Responsible for several departments that perform different functions.
5. Project Managers : Have general management responsibility because they coordinate people across several departments to accomplish a specific project.
#### Managerial Roles
**Interpersonal Roles** : Involve developing and maintaining positive relationships with significant other. 
Types,
1. Figurehead roles - Involves a set of ceremonial duties resulted from the formal authority or the position of manager.
2. Leader Role - Involves behaviors aiming at building relationships with subordinates and includes communicating, motivating, and coaching subordinates.
3. Liaison Role - Involves building and maintaining a network of relationships with people inside and outside the organization.

**Informational Role** : Involve receiving and transmitting information so managers can serve as the nerve centers for their divisions or organizations.
Types,
1. Monitor - Perpetually scanning the environment for information, interrogating liaison contacts and subordinates, and receiving unsolicited information, much of it as a result of the personal contacts.
2. Disseminator - Transmitting information to the relevant party (s) and facilitating to transfer information from one subordinates to another.
3. Spokesperson - Transmitting information by a manager about his unit or the organization representing his unit or organization to outsiders.

**Decision-making Roles** : Pertain to those events about which the manager must make a choice and take action. 
1. Entrepreneur - acts as a initiator, a designer, and an encourager of changes or innovations.
2. **Disturbance Handler** - Taking corrective actions for the unexpected occurrences arising out of poor management or the changes in the manager’s environment.
3. Resource Allocator - Allocating resources including his own time, subordinates’ time, funds, and equipment among various projects and people.
4. Negotiator - Affecting the manager’s areas of responsibility such as negotiating a head of another division in capital budgeting or negotiating a supplier to change price of material 12.
