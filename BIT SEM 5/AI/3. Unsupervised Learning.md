In unsupervised learning, algorithm is given unlabeled data, no predefined outputs or categories. 
the goals is for the model to find patterns, structures or relationships in the data on its own. 
![[Pasted image 20250910192058.png]]
In this example, we give data of patient age and tumor size, but not whether the tumor is being a benign or a malignant. 
And we are don't ask to diagnose whether the tumor is benign or malignant, because we are not given any labels in the dataset.

Instead, algorithm's job is to find some structure/pattern in the unlabeled data. 

Common tasks in unsupervised learning,
1. Clustering : Grouping similar data points.
2. Dimensionality Reduction :  Simplifying data while keeping important features.
3. Anomaly Detection: Finding unusual data points.
