Arthur Samuel is a pioneer in the field of AI and ML, best known for popularizing the term "machine learning" and for earliest self-learning programs. 

Arthur Samuel's checkers game,
Developed in 1950s using an IBM computer. It was a self-learning program that played checkers. The program used a form of reinforcement learning(it played thousands of games, evaluated board positions and improved over time).
It incorporated a **minimax algorithm** with a **heuristic evaluation function**. 

#### Machine learning Algorithms,
The main two types are,
1. Supervised learning(learns from labeled data)
2. Unsupervised learning(learns from unlabeled data)

Other types,
3. semi-supervised learning(small amount of labeled data and a large amount of unlabeled data)
4. Reinforcement learning(learns by interacting with an environment and receiving feedback, reward or penalties)
5. Self-supervised learning(used in modern AI like GPT models)
6. Online learning(learns continuously from streaming data)
7. Ensemble learning(combine multiple models)
