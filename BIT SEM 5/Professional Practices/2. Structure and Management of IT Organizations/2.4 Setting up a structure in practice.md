An organization may have a structure that includes elements of several types of structures. 
Ex: A bespoke software house

- Steps of setting up a structure,
	1. Plan the future
		Make you plans as far in advance as you can.
		For startups: 3-5 years
		Established organizations: 10 or more years
	2. Learn for the past
		Think on what hasn't worked successfully in terms of how teams/departments interact. 
		`If you have a one-story home with a foundational crack, adding more levels will simply make the issue worse`
	3. Build the organizational structure
		Priority should be,
		1. Selecting the ideal processes
		2. Reaching business objectives
		3. Providing good client services
	4. Fill in people
		Determining if an existing employee is naturally suited to the re-defined structure.
	5. Balance authority and responsibility
		Give your staff equal amounts of authority and responsibility regardless of where they sit within the organizational hierarchy.
	6. Fill in employee data and metrices
	7. Practice robust performance management of employees
	8. Review your organizational structure annually
