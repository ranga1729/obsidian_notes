- Common models,
	1. Organic model
	2. The bureaucratic model
	3. Matrix management
#### 1. The Organic Model
- Flexible workplace with a horizontal mode of communication. 
> [!NOTE] Horizontal communication
> Employees share their responsibilities in groups and teams and interact with different departments, managers and colleagues to complete work
- Characteristics,
	1. Cross functional teams
	2. Free flow of information
	3. Wide span of information
	4. Wide span of control
	5. Decentralization
	6. Low formalization
#### 2. The Bureaucratic Model
- Employees have specialized tasks that only they can perform. 
- Common on larger organizations or organizations that have more complex operations. 
- Characteristics,
	1. High specialization
	2. Rigid departmentalization
	3. Governed by precise rules
	4. Clear chain of commands
	5. High formalization
	6. Hierarchical structure
	7. Recruitment is based on qualifications
	8. Employees are protected against arbitrary dismissal
	9. Promotions are based on seniority and achievements
#### 3. The Matrix Management
- Employees are departmentalized based on their functions and on the product of their respective departments. 
- Individuals may be responsible to more than one manager. 
- Characteristics,
	- Individuals have to report to different departments regardless of the part of the company their are belonged to. 
	- Collaboration
	- Fast response to change
