##### Criteria for a professional code of conduct
- Provisions must be in the public interest and consistent with the law of the land.
- Provisions must be generally acceptable to members of the profession.
- Must be precise and making it clear what constitutes a violation. 
- Scope should be limited to professional conduct. 
- Should be accurately worded.

Codes my be applied consistently and disciplinary procedures must enforce fairness. 

##### Distinction between "Code of Conduct" and "Code of Ethics"
- Code of Conduct : more aspirational and less regulatory.
- Code of Ethics : describes codes that are closer to regulatory rules. 
Both differ from _codes of practice_, which focus on good job performance. 

Professional code of conduct primarily address ethical problems and misconduct in the workplace, rather than good practice. 

Ex: Doctors having sexual relationships with patients/taking drugs
