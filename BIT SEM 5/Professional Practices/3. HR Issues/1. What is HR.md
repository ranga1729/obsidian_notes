### Recruitment and Selection
- when staff of a company leaves the company, 
	- Cost of recruiting new staff - high
	- Damage caused by loss of continuity - high
	Therefore, org want to keep staff turnover low.
- HRM means
	Process of employing people, training them, compensating them, developing policies relating to them, and developing strategies to retain them.
- The role of HR Manager
	Oversee the regular duties of the Human Resources (HR) division, including hiring and conducting interviews, paying employees, handling leave requests, and enforcing business regulations and procedures.
- Every manager has some role relating to human resource management. 
- Steps of staffing functions,
	1. Creation of a staffing strategy
		Determine how many to hire according to revenue projection
	2. Creation of guidelines to promote multiculturalism at work
	3. Recruitment 
	4. Selection

#### Selection
- Types of selection techniques,
	1. A series of one-to-one interviews with senior management and senior technical staff
		Very reliable. 
	2. Interview by a Panel
		Researches show its unreliable.
		Tend to favour smooth talkers. 
	3. Assessment of References
	4. Psychometric Tests
		Types,
		- Ability tests - Measure individual's abilities
		- Aptitude tests - Measure a persons potential to learn the skills needed for the job
		- Personality test - Assess the characteristics of a person
	5. Situational Assessment
		Applicants are brought together and put into a variety of situations. 
		Their performance is monitored. 
		(Situational questions - Interviewer describes a scenario to the candidate and asks them what they would do in such circumstances)
	6. Task Assessment
		Candidates are asked to carry out some of the tasks that they will be required to do in the job

### Dismissals
- Types,
	1. Unfair dismissal
		An act of employment termination made without good reason. 
		The law accepts a wide variety of reasons,
		- Lack of capability
		- Misconduct
		- Breach of the law
		- Redundancy
	2. Constructive dismissal
		An employee with at least 2 years' continuous service in circumstances where they feel forced to resign because of a fundamental breach of contract on the part of their employer. 
	3. Wrongful dismissal
		Employee's contract of employment has been terminated by the employer, where the termination breaches one or more terms of the contract of employment, or a statute provision or rule in employment law
