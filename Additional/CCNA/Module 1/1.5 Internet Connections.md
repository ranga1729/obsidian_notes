#### Home and Small Office Internet connections
![[Pasted image 20251209113544.png]]
Common connection types,
1. Cable
	Typically offered by cable television providers.
	Internet data signals transmit over the same cable. 
	Provides high bandwidth, high availability, always-on connection to the internet.
2. DSL(Digital Subscriber Line)
	Runs over a telephone line. 
	ADSL(Asymmetrical Digital Subscriber Line) - download speed is higher than the upload speed. 
	Provides high bandwidth, high availability, always-on connection to the internet.
3. Cellular
	Uses cell phone network to connect. 
	Performance is limited the capabilities of the phone and the connected cell tower.
4. Satellite 
	Satellite dishes require a clear line of sight to the satellite. 
5. Dial-up Telephone
	Uses a phone line and a modem.
	Provides a low bandwidth and not suitable for large data transfer.
#### Business Internet Connections
business require higher & dedicated bandwidth and a managed service.

Common types,
1. Dedicated Leased Lines
	Reserved circuits within the service providers network. 
	Rented at a monthly or yearly rate. 
2. Metro Ethernet
	Known as Ethernet WAN.
	Extends LAN access technology into the WAN.
3. Business DSL
	The popular choice is SDSL(Symmetric Digital Subscriber Line), the consumer version of DSL.. 
	Provides upload and download at the same high speed. 
4. Satellite
#### The Converging Network
- Traditional Separate Networks
	Back then, data networks, telephone networks and video networks for television as separate networks. These couldn't communicate to each other, each network used different technologies to carry signal and used its own set of rules and standards. 
![[Pasted image 20251209115333.png]]
- Converged Networks
	Nowadays, these separate data, telephone and video networks converges. They can deliver data, voice and video between different devices over the same network, uses the same set of rules and standards.
