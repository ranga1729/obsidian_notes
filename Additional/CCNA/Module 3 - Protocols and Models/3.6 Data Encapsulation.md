### Segmenting
Segmenting is the process of breaking up messages into smaller units. 
Multiplexing is the process of taking multiple streams of segmented data and interleaving them together. 

Segmenting message has two primary benefits, 
- Increases speed: Small data can be easily sent over an network rather than handling a large data chunk at once.
- Increases efficiency: Only segments fail to reach the destination need to be re-transmitted, not the entire data stream. 

### Sequencing
Process of numbering the segments so the message may be re-assembled at the destination. TCP is responsible for sequencing the individual segments.

### Protocol Data Unit(PDU) 
As application data is passed down the protocol stack on its way to be transmitted across the network media, various protocol information is added at each level. 
The form that a piece of data takes at any layer is called a "Protocol Data Unit".
At each stage of the process,  a PDU has a different name to reflect it new function.

- If the Transport layer protocol is TCP, its called a "Segment".
- If the Transport layer protocol is UDP, its called a "Datagram".

### De-Encapsulation
Data is de-encapsulated as they moves up the stack.
When a layer completes its process, that layer strips off its header and passes it up to the next level to be processed.
