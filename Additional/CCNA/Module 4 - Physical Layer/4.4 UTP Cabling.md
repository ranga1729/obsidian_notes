Has 4 pairs of color-coded copper wires twisted together and encased in a flexible plastic sheath.
No shielding is used. 

Steps takes to limit crosstalk,
1. Cancellation : Each wire in a pair of wires uses opposite polarity. One wire is negative, the other wire is positive. They are twisted together and the magnetic fields effectively cancel each other and outside EMI/RFI.
2. Each wire is twisted a different amount, which helps prevent crosstalk amongst the wires in the cable.

#### UTP Cabling Standards
UTP cable standards are set by TIA/EIA(TIA/EIA 568). 
Standardizing elements are.
- Cable types
- Cable Length
- Connectors
- Cable Terminations
- Testing Methods

Copper cable standards are established by the IEEE. 
They rates cables by their performance.
- Category 3
- Category 5/5e
- Category 6S

Types of cables,
1. Straight through UTP cables : Connect unlike devices (like a computer to a switch) with identical pinouts on both ends
2. Crossover UTP Cable : . Crossover UTP cables connect like devices directly (like a computer to a computer) by swapping the transmit and receive wire pairs
