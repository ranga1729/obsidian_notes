Physical connection can be wired or wireless. Not all physical connection offer the same level of performance. 

What's happening in the Physical layer ?
- The physical layer accepts a complete frame from the data link layer.
- Encodes the frame as a series of signals that are transmitted to the local media. This signal can be electrical, optical or radio waves that represent bits in each frame. 
- These signal are then send over the media **one at a time.** 
- The destination node physical layer retrieves these individual signal from the media, restores them to their bit representations. 
- Then it passes the bits up to the data link layer as a complete frame.
