# Mobile Design
Goal: Provide uses with a seamless, intuitive and enjoyable experience on their mobile devices. 

Importance of mobile design,
- Designers need to keep update with new technologies and devices.
- Mobile users have high expectations for the quality and performance of the app. 
- User centric designs
- Give users a good first impression. 
- High quality designs can increase the users engagements. 
- Mobile designs require a balance between creativity and understanding the limitation of devices.
- Designers must find a way to create the best possible experience within these constraints. 

# Elements of Mobile Design
Elements in mobile designs are,
1. Context
2. Message
3. Look and feel
4. Layout
5. Color
6. Typography
7. Graphics

### 1. Context
The environment, circumstances, and conditions surrounding users when they interact with a mobile app or website. 

Why the context matter ?
- It influences user behavior, preferences, and expectations
- It affect usability, engagement and satisfaction.
- Context tailors design to meet user needs and enhance their experience. 

Key aspects to consider in context:
- Physical environment(location, noise, lighting, ...)
- Device characteristics(screen size, input methods, network speed, ...)
- User's goal, motivations and emotional state 
- Socio-cultural factors(language, cultural norms, accessibility needs)

Key questions to pay attention:
- Who are the users ? (understand their behavior and assumptions)
- What is happening ? (consider circumstances for content absorption)
- When will they interact ? (identify user's available timeframe)
- Where are the users ? (public/private or indoor/outdoor places)
- Why will they use your app ? (identify the value they gain)
- How are they using their mobile devices ? (device position and orientation)

### 2. Message
The message is the overall impression you create through visual design. 
The important thing is not the designer's opinion, its the opinion of the user.
The design should convey the right message to the user in the right context. 

### 3. Look and Feel
Refers to the appearance and style of a design. 
Ex: being clean, modern, usable

Its can be subjective and involves both visual and tactile elements. 
This can have a significant impact in user exp, influencing how users interact with the design and the emotional response it generates. 

Style guides can serve as a reference point for designers and stakeholders to ensure that the design remains consistent and aligned with the overall design goals

### 4. Layout
Layout affect readability, hierarchy and overall user exp. 

Importance of layout:
- Addressing subjective nature of design. 
- Avoiding unnecessary layout changes due to feedback. 

Different devices have different screen sizes and navigation methods. Layouts need to be adapted accordingly to maintain consistency.

Fixed layouts have a set number of pixels while Fluid layouts can scale to the full width of the screen regardless of the orientation.
### 5. Color
Color depth refers to the number of bits representing the color of a single pixel in a digital image.
Greater color depth means more colors, making an image more vivid and accurate.

Standard bit depths:
- 1 bit - 21 = 2 shades(Black and White)
- 2 bits - 22 = 4 shades(shades of gray)
- 8 bit - 28 = 256 shades
- 10 bits - 210 = 1024 shades
- 12 bits - 212 = 4096 shades
- 24 bits - 224 = 16,777,216 shades

Different devices have different color depths, which affect the quality of displayed images.
The ideal color depth depends on the data storage on the device and the internet bandwidth.

Benefits of high depths,
- Improved color quality. 
- Better precision and color transitions. 

Limitation of high depth:
- Images/videos with high color depth need more space and bandwidth. 
- Often require compression to reduce file size. 

Psychology behind color in mobile design:
- People respond to different colors in different ways. 
- Different colors evoke different emotions.
- Colors can be used to deliver right message and expectations.
- Colors can have opposing meaning in different cultures. It's important to consider cultural context when designing for global audience.


Defining color palettes is important for maintaining a consistent use of color in your mobile design.
Color palettes consist of a predefined number of colors to use throughout the design.

Basic ways to define a color palette:
- Sequential
- Adaptive
- Inspired

**Sequential Color Palettes**
- Consist of primary, secondary, and tertiary colors. 
- Primary color is often reserved as the "brand" color. 
- The secondary and tertiary colors are complementary colors selected using a color wheel.
- Useful for maintaining consistency throughout the design

**Adaptive Color Palettes**
- Leverages the most common colors present in a supporting graphic or image.
- Used when creating a design that is meant to look native on the device to ensure that the colors are consistent with the target mobile platform. 
- Useful for creating designs that blend seamlessly with the user interface of the device.

**Inspired Color Palettes**
- Created from the colors found in great pieces of design, both online and offline.
- Colors are extracted from the source image or object, but the source material should never be used in a design.
- Useful for creating unique and visually appealing designs that are not limited to the traditional color scheme.

### 6. Typography
The technique of arranging type to make written language legible, readable and appealing when displayed.
Two approaches on how type is rendered on a mobile screen :
1. subpixel-based screen
2. Greater pixel density or pixel per inch(PPI)

**2. Greater pixel density or pixel per inch(PPI)**
Technique of having large pixel density to increase the sharpness of the screen.
Pixel density = dividing the width of the display area in pixels by the width of the display area in inches
![[Pasted image 20260519150706.png]]

**2. Sub-Pixel Based screens**
Subpixel = division of each pixel into a red, green, blue(RGB) unit at a microscopic level. 
Good for antialiasing for each font character. 
![[Pasted image 20260519150933.png]]
Ex: Microsoft Windows mobile platform used the subpixel technique

The higher the density of pixels, the sharper the screen appears to the naked eye.

Anti-aliased text on a screen with a high density of tiny pixels appears sharper to the eye

Some mobile screens have both a high PPI and subpixel technology, though these are unnecessary together.

Best practices:
- Use legible fonts to make it easy to read. 
- Choose appropriate fonts for the content and tone of the app. 
- Use font sizes large enough to read comfortably. 
- Contrast between the text and the background must be sufficient. 
- Provide good leading and line spacing.
- Utilize headings and short paragraphs. 
- Provide some spacing between the right and left side of the screen edge. 

### 7. Graphic 
Used to establish visual exp.
Iconography is the most common form of graphics used in mobile designs. 
I cons should be clear and easy to understand. 
Symbols and metaphors can make the meaning of the icon. 

Best practices:
- Don't overwhelm users with graphics. 
- Keep graphics simple and easy to understand. 
- Test graphics on different devices to ensure they view correctly. 
- Optimize graphics to load quickly.
