## Static Methods
Primary objective : improve the quality of software products by assisting engineers to recognize and fix their own defects early in the software development process.

Types of defects can be identified,
- Deviation from standards
- Missing Requirements
- Design defects
- Non-maintainable code
- Inconsistence interface specifications

Example coding defects,
- Unused variables
- Dead code
- Infinite loop
- Variables with undefined values
- Wrong syntax

Testable featured with static testing,
- BRD(Business Requirement Document)
- Functional or System requirements
- Unit use cases
- Prototype
- Prototype specification document
- Test data
- DB fields/Dictionary spread sheets
- Documentation/Training guides/user manual
- Test cases/Test plan strategy document
- Traceability matrix document
- Performance test script/automation

##### Review
Refers to the thorough examination of supporting documents, such as software requirements specifications, to identify and correct errors and defects.
Aims to identify possible flaws in the software design.

The review process can be,
- Informal - The goal is improve the quality of the work. Informal reviews are not documented.
	Ex: buddy checking, pair review, pairing
- Formal - During project planning, project manager should allow time for formal reviews
	Ex: Walkthrough, Technical review, Inspection

Static testing tools, 
- Source meter
- PyCharm

Benefits of Statis testing,
- Identify errors in the early phase of development. Reduced cost of re-work. 
- Reduced development time
- At later phases, there will be fewer errors to detect.
- Code becomes more maintainable. 
- Give developers a better idea of the quality of the issues found in the software. 
- Automated tools make testing easy
- Boot the communication between teams. 

### Informal Review
Characters,
- Main purpose: detecting potential defects
- Additional purposes: generate new ideas or solutions
- Doesn't based on formal review process
- May not involve review meeting
- May be performed by a colleague of the author
- Results may be documented
- Varies in usefulness depending on the reviewer(s).
- Use of checklists is optional.
- Very commonly used in Agile development.

An informal review may simply be one person saying to a colleague, 
	'Could you have a quick look at what I've just done?'
Then the colleague may spend some time and giving any comments back to the author, such as typos, something missing, or a 'But have you thought of this?' comment.

With the right person reviewing, this buddy check can be very effective (and at little cost in time). 

Pair working = one person works with another to produce a work product while the second person continually evaluating what the first person is typing.

### Walkthroughs
Main purpose: find defects, improve the software product, consider alternative implementations, evaluate conformance to standards and specifications.
Additional purposes: exchanging ideas about techniques or style variations, training of participants. achieving consensus.

Review meeting is typically led by the author of the work product.
Use of a scribe is mandatory. 
Used to transfer knowledge and educate a wider audience about a particular work product.
Useful for higher-level work products, such as requirement specifications and architectural documents.

### Technical Reviews
Main purpose: gaining consensus, detecting potential defect
Additional purposes: evaluating quality and building confidence in the work product, generating new ideas, motivating and enabling authors to improve future work products, considering alternative implementations.

Reviewers should be technical peers of the author, and technical experts in relevant disciplines.
Individual preparation before the review meeting is required.
Scribe is mandatory. 

Often a discussion meeting that focuses on achieving consensus about the technical content of a work product that all the participants have studied before the meeting.

Technical reviews are typically less formal than inspections, but generally more formal than walkthroughs. In practice, technical reviews may vary from quite informal to very formal.

### Inspections
Main purpose: detecting potential defects, evaluating quality and building confidence in the work product. preventing future similar defects through author learning and root cause analysis.

Additional purposes: motivating and enabling authors to improve future work products

A defined process is followed, with formal documented outputs, based on rules and checklists.

Individual preparation before the review meeting is required.
Inspection is the most formal review type.

In the inspection meeting, the defects found are logged and any discussion is postponed until the discussion part of the meeting.
