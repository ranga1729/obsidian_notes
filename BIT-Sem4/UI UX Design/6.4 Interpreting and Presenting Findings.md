Presenting styles,
1. Using structured notations
2. Using stories
3. Summarizing

##### 1. Structured notations
Structured notations have been developed to analyze, capture and present information for interaction design.
Ex: UML(Unified Modeling Language)
- Advantage : provides clear guidance on what to look for in the data and what to highlight and that it enforces precision in expression.
- Disadvantage : de-emphasizes or ignores other aspects, and the precision expressed by the notation may be lost on an audience if they don’t know the notation well.
##### 2. Stories - AKA narratives or scenarios
Storytelling is an easy and intuitive approach for people to communicate ideas and experiences.
Storytelling can be used in three different ways,
1. Participants may have told stories of their own during data gathering.
2. stories (or narratives) based on observation, such as ethnographic field studies, may be employed for further data gathering
3. stories may be constructed from smaller snippets or repeated episodes that are found in the data.
##### 3. Summarizing the finding
Presentation styles will usually be used in combination to produce a summary of the findings.
Tables of numerical data may be represented as graphs, diagrams, or rigorous notations, together with workflows or quotations.
