#### Remote Conversation(Virtual Communication)
Communicating at a distance via electronic tools that let you corresponding with people outside of face-to-face communication. 
Ex:
	Telephone conversations
	Emails
	Video conferencing
	Online chats
#### Conversations, Co-Presence and Social Engagement
- **Co-Presence** : Supporting people in their activities when interacting in the same physical space. 
- The motivation is to enable co-located groups to collaborate more effectively when working, learning, and socializing.
