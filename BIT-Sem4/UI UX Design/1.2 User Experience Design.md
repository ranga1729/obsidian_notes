### Usability, User Interface and User Experience
HCI overlaps with areas such as,
- User centered design(UCD),
- User Interface(UI) design,
- User Experience(UX) design

#### User Experience Design(UXD)
User Experience Design(UXD) focuses on,
- Enhancing the end-user's interaction with the company, it's products and services.
- Understanding users needs, values, abilities and limitations.
- Aligning design with business goals and objectives.
- Improving the quality of user interaction and the perception of a product.

#### UI design vs UX design
- UI : 
	A human centered approach to design the aesthetic experience of a product.
	Focus on series of screens, pages, and visual elements.
	Ex: buttons, icons
	UI deign complements user experience. 
- UX: 
	A human centered approach to product design.
	Focus on the internal experience that a person has as they interact with every aspect of a product and services. 

**A successful product is a result of good UI and UX.**

- Consider **Why**, **What** an **How** of product use.

- Considering backgrounds,
	Visual design,

 - Job includes,
	 User research,
	 Creating personas,
	 Design wireframes and interactive prototypes

**WHY** : Clarify the needs and emotions involved in an activity.
**WHAT** : Clarify the functionality.
	The things people can do through this interactive product.
**HOW** : Addresses acting through an object on an operational, sensory-motor level.
	Ex: button press, knob turn, menu navigation

UX is user centered. 
User centered design is iterative process where you take an understanding of the users and their context as a starting point for all design and development.
![[Pasted image 20250319160742.png]]
User centered development process,
1. Understand the context in which users may use a system.
2. Identify and specify the user's requirements. 
3. Develop design solutions. 
4. Evaluate the outcome against the user's context and requirements.
Make further iterations until the evaluation results are satisfactory.

Advantages of user centered development(UCD),
- Leads to sales increase and reduce the cost.
- Designers tailor products for people in specific context and specific tasks, leads to lower human errors.
- Designers can recognize the diversity of cultures and human values.

#### Accommodating Human Diversity
Human could be diverse based on their abilities and disabilities.
This is has a impact on of their accessibility of computing needs.

- Accessibility
	Design of application in a way that it is accessible to disables or otherwise abled people.

Types of disabilities,
1. Visual disabilities
2. Auditory disabilities
3. Motor disabilities
4. Cognitive disabilities

**Visual Disabilities**
Ex: Long/short-sightedness, blindness, colorblindness
Solutions: 
- Braille keyboard, 
- Speech software that reads web pages and documents,
- Screen magnifiers
- Color palettes for color blind users
	![[Pasted image 20250319233712.png]]

**Auditory Disabilities**
Affects the hearing and come in varying degrees of severity up to total deafness.
Solutions,
- Include a written version of audio materials.
- Offer transcriptions for audio files.
- Captions for videos.

**Motor Disabilities**
Problems with the mobility and use of the hands and arms.
Solutions,
- Speech inputs
- Specially designed keyboards
- Eye tracking devices

**Cognitive Disabilities**
Limitations in mental functioning and skills such as communicating, taking care if themselves, social skills.
Ex: Learning disabilities such as Dyslexia, Autism
Solutions,
- Focus on readable content.
- Use a simpler language.
- Visualize the structure of a web using a sitemap.
- Users with dyslexia may have problems reading long pages. By selecting words as hypertext anchors will help these users.
- Include spelling checkers for users with spelling disabilities. 

Example products,
1. Braillesurf from Braillenet
	Included in windows 95, 98, NT, 2000
	Speech, Braille large texts.
2. BrookesTalk
	Under development by Oxford Brookes university focuses on facilitating intelligent web-searching. Speech output, screen-magnification available.
3. EIAD
	A browser form Sarsfield solutions which provides enhancements specifically for people with special needs and learning difficulties. 
	Touch-screen, Simplified language interface available.
