Goal : Improve the artifact's design.
Evaluation focuses on,
- Usability
- User experience
It involves all aspect of the user interaction with the product. 
A range from low-tech prototypes to complete systems can be evaluated. 

Evaluation can be take place on,
1. Lab environments : Characteristics(ex: web accessibility)
2. In a natural setting(wild studies) : User experience aspects
3. Remote studies : online behavior

Evaluation time can be depend on the type of product and the development process being followed. 
Two types,
1. Formative evaluation
	evaluations are conducted during design.
	check that a product continues to meet users’ needs
2. Summative evaluation
	Evaluations carried out to assess the success of a finished product
