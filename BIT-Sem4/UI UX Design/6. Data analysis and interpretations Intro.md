2. Quantitative analysis

Data analysis process can be begins with the initial reactions.
Ex: 
	Identifying patterns, 
	Calculating values such as ratios, averages, percentages
	Look for anomalies and clean them.(Data cleansing/cleaning)
	(ex: people who are 999 years old)

> [!NOTE] Data Cleansing/Cleaning
> Data cleansing is the process of identifying and correcting errors or inconsistencies in a dataset to improve its quality and reliability. This ensure a trustworthy analysis results. 
> This includes,
> - Remove duplicate records
> - Fixing incorrect or inconsistent data(ex: typos, formatting issues)
> - Handling missing values
> - Filtering out irrelevant or outdated information

A common mistake happened in data analysis is mixing investigator's existing beliefs or biases to influence the interpretation of results. 

Ex: A analysis has been done on the inquiries of a customer care center. 
It has revealed that inquiries routed through the Colombo office take longer to process than those routed through the Jaffna office. 

Possible interpretations, 
- Colombo customer care officer are less efficient. 
- Colombo officers provide more detailed responses. 
- Technology used in Colombo office needs to be updated.
- Colombo office's demand is higher than other places.
