Analytical frameworks helps analyze and interpret data from a qualitative study.

Approaches,
1. Conversational Analysis
2. Discourse Analysis
3. Content Analysis
4. Interaction Analysis
5. Grounded Theory
6. System based frameworks

##### 1. Conversational Analysis
- Focuses on the structure, patterns, semantics of a conversation in fine details. (ex: starting, turn-taking, pauses, interruptions)
- Used to understand how people use language in social interactions. 
- Used in sociological studies.
Ex: Analyzing doctor-patient conversations to see how diagnoses are delivered.
##### 2. Discourse Analysis
- Examine how language is used in written or spoken communication to shape meaning and power. 
- Strongly interpretive.
- Pays great attention to context.
- Goes beyond words to explore cultural and social contexts.
Ex: Studying political speeches to uncover hidden ideologies.
##### 3. Content Analysis
- Content analysis typically involves classifying the data into themes or categories and then studying the frequency of category occurrences.
- Often used to find patterns, frequencies, or themes in large volumes of data.
Ex: Counting how many times certain words appear in news articles. 
##### 4. Interaction Analysis
- Investigates how people interact with each other and their environment, often using video recordings.
- Focuses on gestures, timing and coordination.
- This is an inductive process and focuses on both talks and non-verbal interaction with artifacts and technologies. 
Ex: Studying teamwork in a classroom.
##### 5. Grounded Theory
- Aims to develop a new theory directly from the data using a systematic analysis. 
- This is an inductive approach to develop a theory.
- Uses iterative coding(data categorization) and constant comparison of data to build theory from the ground up. 
- Data coding aspects : Open coding, Axial coding, Selective coding
Ex: Interviewing people about coping with illness and developing a model of emotional adjustments.
##### 6. Systems based Frameworks
- Looks at data within broader systems. (social, organizational, technological)
- Analyze how different components influence each other. 
- Two such frameworks,
	1. Socio-technical theory
	2. Distributed Cognition of Teamwork
Ex: Understanding how hospital policies, staff roles, and patient feedback interact in healthcare delivery.
