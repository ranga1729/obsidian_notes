### Conceptual Designs
- Conceptual design is the early stage of the design process where the overall idea, structure, and user experience of a system or product are planned without focusing on technical details.
- This will emerged from the understanding of the problem space and the current functional requirements.

**Interface Metaphors**
Metaphors combine new knowledge with familiar knowledge in a way that will help users understand the product. 
**Purpose**: To make interfaces more intuitive by linking them to things users already know.

Ex: 
	Trash bin icon for deleting file, 
	shopping cart for e-commerce websites

Once the metaphors are identified, they need to be evaluated,
- How much structure does the metaphor provide?
- How much of the metaphor is relevant to the problem?
- Is the interface metaphor easy to represent?
- Will your audience understand the metaphor? 
- How extensible is the metaphor? 
- Does it have extra aspects that may be useful later?

**Interaction Types**
Interaction types describe **how users engage** with the system or product.
Types,
1. Instructing : Giving commands (ex: clicking a button)
2. Conversing : Dialogues (ex: voice assistant)
3. Manipulating : Direct interaction with objects (ex: dragging and dropping)
4. Exploring : Navigating through spaces (ex: scrolling)

Type of interaction is best suited to the current design depends on the domain and the type of product being developed.
Ex: 
	computer games : manipulating style
	software application : instructing 

### Concrete Design
Concrete design is the stage where the **visual and interactive details** of a product are finalized, based on the earlier conceptual design.
- Focuses on the **look and feel**: colors, typography, buttons, icons, spacing
- Defines **actual screen layouts**, UI elements, and interactions
- Results in **high-fidelity mockups** or prototypes ready for development

Considerations of a concrete design,
- Visual appearance
- Interface type, guidelines, principles, rules
- Issues (accessibility and inclusiveness, cultural designs)

#### Participatory Design
Participatory design is an approach where all the stakeholders i.e. employees, customers, end-users, partners, designers, and researchers are actively involved in the design process.
#### Design Thinking
![[Pasted image 20250511102941.png]]
**Design thinking** is a **problem-solving approach** focused on understanding users, challenging assumptions, and creating innovative solutions through iterative design.

Key features,
- **User-centered**: Puts user needs and experiences first
- **Collaborative**: Encourages teamwork across disciplines
- **Iterative**: Involves constant testing and improvement

Stages,
1. **Empathize** – Understand the user's needs
2. **Define** – Clearly state the problem
3. **Ideate** – Brainstorm creative solutions
4. **Prototype** – Build simple versions of solutions
5. **Test** – Try them with users and refine
