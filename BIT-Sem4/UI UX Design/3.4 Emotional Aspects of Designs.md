#### Emotional design
Emotional design is the concept of how to create designs that evoke emotions which result in positive user experiences.
Concerned with what makes people feel happy, sad, annoyed, anxious, frustrated or motivated.

Ex:
	Advertising Agencies : Showing a picture of a cute animal or a child with hungry, big eyes on a website. 

UX designers must address three levels of cognitive responses when design,
1. Visceral : User's first impression of the design
2. Behavioral : Users subconsciously evaluate how your design helps them achieve goals, and how easily.
3. Reflective : After they encounter your design, users will consciously judge its performance and benefits, including value for money

Designers use several features to make an interface expressive, 
- Sound
- Color
- Shapes
- Icons
- Virtual agents
These helps to create an emotional connection with users.
Ex: 
	Animated icons : A recycle bin expanding when a file is deleted. 

#### Affective Computing
Affective computing refers to use computers to recognize and express emotions in the same way as humans.

Ex: Emotional AI : Analyze real-time face expression to measure feelings, voice and behaviors.
Helps to forecast what people buy when they feel sad, bored or happy.
#### Persuasive Technology
Techniques to draw user's attention to certain kinds of information in an attempt to change what they do or think. 
Ex:
	Pop-up ads
	Warning messages
	Reminders, 
	Prompts
	Personalize messages
