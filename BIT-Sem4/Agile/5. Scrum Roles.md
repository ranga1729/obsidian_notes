#### Scrum Rules
1. Teams: of 7-9 members, cross functional.
2. Time-boxes
3. Feedback and learning through frequent delivery
4. Predictability
5. Collective and adaptive planning to build the right product
6. Improve productivity and quality by
	reducing waste
	making team focus on time-boxes 

#### Scrum Roles
1. Product Owner
2. SCRUM Master
3. Development Team

### 1. Product Owner
Responsible for maximizing the value of the product by managing the **Product Backlog** and ensuring that the development team builds the right feature.
Act as a bridge between stakeholders and the development team.

The product owner must understand the need and priorities of the stakeholder to act as their representative. 

Product owner act as,
- Product manager
- Business analyst
- Feature tester

Responsibilities,
- Share the product vision/goals with the team.
- Identify the requirements 
- Prioritize requirements
- Do clarification on requirements if someone want to
- Provide feedbacks
- Negotiate with stakeholders
- Manage economics.
- Participate in planning.
- Collaborate with the development team and the stakeholders.
- Define acceptance criteria and verify them.

**Product owner team ???**
Product owner role can be handle by a team instead of a single person due to heavy workload. If so, the product owner can delegate some product owner responsibilities to other people as long as there is one person on the team who is the decision maker.

Types of Product owners(represent different roles when the traditional Product Owner (PO) role is split across multiple teams or organizational levels in large-scale Scrum implementations.),
1. Product owner - proxy
	Act as an intermediary between the real Product owner and the development team.
2. Chief Product Owner
	Found in large organizations with multiple teams working on a single product. 
	Oversees multiple product owners, ensuring alignment across teams. 
	Manage the high-level product vision.
3. Product line owner
	Responsible for an entire production line that consist of multiple related products. 
4. Feature Product Owner
	Focuses on specific feature or components of a product.

Other points,
- PO does the release planning.
- Define sprint goals.
- Creating and refining requirements.
- Requirement estimations and prioritizing. 
- Define acceptance criteria/acceptance tests for backlog items. 
- Closely work with stakeholders and the dev team.
- Every scrum team must have a product owner.

### 2. Scrum Master
Scrum master's responsibilities are designed to ensure that the scrum process is followed and that the ream works effectively within the Agile principles. 

Responsibilities,
- Coach the team on SCRUM principles. 
	for both the dev team and the product owner
	ensure the team adhere to the scrum values.
- Remove obstacles faced by the team.
- Assist the team in achieving the iteration goals.
- Act as a 'servant-leader' to the team.
- Protect the development team from outside interferences.

Scrum master doesn't have the authority that a project manager would have,
- The scrum master doesn't hire or fire.
- Can not dictate the team what task to do or how to do them.
- Nor responsible for making sure the work gets done.
