#### 1. Portfolio planning
Overseeing a collection of projects or products, aligning them with the overall strategy of the organization.
Goal :
	Maximize the value,
	Balance risk
	Ensure important tasks are prioritized
	Determine which projects to fund
- In here, backlog item can be an entire product/project or a product increment.
- Portfolio planning is a never ending activity and the output of product-level is an input to portfolio planning.
- Participants,
	Internal stakeholders
	Product owners of individual product
	Senior architects and technical leads
- Outputs,
	Portfolio backlog
	Active products
- Activities
	1. Scheduling
	2. Managing inflows
	3. Managing outflows
	4. Managing in-process products

**1. Scheduling strategies**
- Optimize for lifecycle profits.
- Calculate the cost of delay.
- Estimate for accuracy, not precision.
**2. Inflow strategies**
Inflow refers to new initiatives proposing, evaluation, and select for inclusion in the portfolio. 
- Apply the economic filter
- Balance the arrival rate with the departure rate
- Quickly embrace emergent opportunities
- Plan for smaller, more frequent releases.
**3. Outflow strategies**
Outflow refers to how initiatives are completed, paused, or removed from the portfolio.
- Focus on idle work, not idle workers.
- Establish a WIP limit.(Work in Progress)
- Wait for a complete team.
**4. In-process strategies**
Use marginal economics.
Options,
- Preserve : Continue developing the product.
- Deliver : Stop working on the product and ship it.
- Pivot : Take the learning and change the direction.
- Terminate : Stop working on the product and kill it.
