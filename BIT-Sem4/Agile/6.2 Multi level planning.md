Multi level planning is the process of planning at multiple levels of details and at multiple times throughout development.

Levels of planning,
- Portfolio
- Product
- Release
- Sprint
- Daily

#### 1. Portfolio planning
Determine which product to work on, in what order, and for how long.
#### 2. Product planning
Product vision : Clear description of the areas in which the stakeholders get value.
High-level product backlog. 
Product roadmap.
