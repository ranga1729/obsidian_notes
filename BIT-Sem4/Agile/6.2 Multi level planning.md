Multi level planning is the process of planning at multiple levels of details and at multiple times throughout development.

Levels of planning,
- Portfolio
- Product
- Release
- Sprint
- Daily
*****

#### 4. Sprint Planning
Determine the specific product backlog items that the Scrum team will work on in the next sprint(usually 1-4 weeks).
#### 5. Daily Planning
Managing tasks and priorities on a daily basis within the context of longer-term goals (like sprints or releases).
It includes checking progress, adjusting tasks, and managing dependencies between levels of planning.
