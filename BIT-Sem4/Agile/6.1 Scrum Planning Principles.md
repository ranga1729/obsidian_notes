Traditional projects creates a detailed plan upfront before development work begin. 
SCRUM does not believe it can be done in very beginning. 
Reason,
- Can't produce all of the planning artifacts up front.

Principles,
1. Keep important options open until you have more information to take critical decisions. 
2. Up-front planning is ok as long as you are adaptable for upcoming changes.
3. Frequent re-planning
4. Use validated learning to continuously produce better plans.
5. Creating large unfinalized artifacts might be wasteful.
6. Forms of waste,
	1. Wasted effort
	2. Waste of having to update plans
	3. wasted opportunities.
7. Favor smaller, frequent releases.
	- Faster feedbacks.
	- Improves ROI
8. Plan to learn fast and pivot when necessary.
