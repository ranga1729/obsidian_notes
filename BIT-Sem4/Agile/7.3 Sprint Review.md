During the sprint review, we inspect the result.
At the end of the sprint, team conduct two meetings,
1. The sprint review
2. The sprint retrospective
#### Sprint Review Meeting
**team demonstrates** the work completed to **stakeholders** and gets **feedback**. The goal is to inspect the increment and adapt the product backlog if needed.

During the review,
- Provides a summary of what has and has not been accomplished.
- A demonstration of the increment.
- Discuss the current state of the product, and the future product direction.

Output : A groomed product backlog and an updated release plan.
Possible issues of a sprint review,
- Getting timely approval or formal acceptance from stakeholders for completed work, which can delay progress or deployment.
- Inconsistent participation by stakeholders or team members, leading to poor feedback, misalignment, or lack of accountability.
- Presenting overly large or complex features in one sprint review, which can overwhelm the session, make demos ineffective, and reduce meaningful discussion.

#### Sprint Retrospective Meeting
Retrospective is an **internal team meeting** to reflect on 
	what went **well**, 
	what could be **improved**, and 
	how to **enhance processes** for the next sprint.

Outputs,
- Improvement actions that the team has agreed to perform in the next sprint.
- A backlog of insights collected during the current retrospective that the team will not address in the upcoming sprint but might choose to address in the future.
- Improved fellowship between team members.

Possible Issues,
- Low attendance.
- All fluff, no stuff.
- Ignoring the elephant in the room.
- Poor facilitator.
- Depressing and energy draining 
- Blame game 
- Complaint session 
- Replaces ad hoc process improvement 
- Too ambitious 
- No follow-through
