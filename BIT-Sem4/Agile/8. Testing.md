- In traditional process models(ex: waterfall), testing is a separate phase.
- In Agile models, testing is performed alongside the development. 
- Agile testing follows the agile manifesto and applies the principles of agile.
- In Scrum, quality isn't something a testing team tests in at the end. It's something that a cross-functional scrum team owns and continuously builds in and verifies every sprint. 
- Agile testing reduce the need for late testing. 
- Agile testers performs continuous, parallel testing at each sprint. 

Critical factors in Agile testing, 
1. Test automation : Sufficiently automate test cases. 
2. Exploratory testing : Manual testing conduct without a lot of preparation. Explore the application to find faults and bugs. 
3. Independent testing is not allowed : Each developer should contribute in testing. External testers also can be joined but they must act as a part of the agile team. 
4. If multiple teams are working on the same project, system-wide testing is a must. Test managers and full time testes assigned to the team should exchange their views. 

Traditional Quality assurance tools,
- Measures can be either
	1. Constructive : Proactive steps taken to build into the product from the beginning. (ex: coding standards)
	2. Analytical : Reactive steps taken to detect and analyze defects after or during the development. (ex: unit, system, integration testing)
- A QA plan is drafted by the QA manager.
- Separate QA group is there. 
- Decide when and how tests are performed in advance.

Agile Quality assurance tools,
- Entire team is responsible for the product and it's quality. 
- No dedicated QA group.
- Quality assurance in an agile team is based on the principle of “Inspection and Adaptation"
- Don't decide when and how plans are performed in advance, instead decisions are made in sprint's meetings. (sprint plan, daily scrum, review)
- QA issues are discussed in daily scrum.

Advantages of Agile QA measures,
- Every development artifact is tested. (program codes, diagram, designs, backlog)
- Corrective measures are taken place early as possible. 
- Each team member follow standards(ex: clean code, test automation, test first approach)
- Immediate actions can be taken according to the needs. 

- Definition of Read(DOR) :  A checklist used to assure the quality of a User Story. If a given User Story is not clear enough to draft a test case (if the tester cannot figure out when to pass or fail a test case) we can say the story is not sufficiently refined.
- Definition of Done(DOD) : Another checklist used by the team to decide whether a given feature is ready to be included in the Sprint Review.

- In traditional approach : tests are handled by a separate testing team
- In agile testing : team themselves perform tests
