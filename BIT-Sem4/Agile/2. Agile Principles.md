Agile principles,
- Make organizations robust & antifragile. 
- Make development process and organization robust and antifragile. 
Agile principles are organized into several categorized,
1. Variability & uncertainty
2. Prediction & adaptation
3. Validated learning
4. Work in process(WIP)
5. Progress
6. Performance

### 1. Variability and Uncertainty
Four principles,
1. Embrace helpful variability
2. Employ iterative and incremental development
3. Leverage variability through inspection, adaptation and transparency.
4. Reduce all forms of uncertainty simultaneously.
