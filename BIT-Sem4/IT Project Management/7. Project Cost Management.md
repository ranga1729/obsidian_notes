**Cost** : A resource sacrificed or foregone to achieve a specific objective; typically measured in monetary units
**Project Cost Management**: Processes required to ensure project completion within approved budget
#### Project cost management process,
1. **Estimating costs** : Developing approximations of resource costs needed for project completion
2. **Determining the budget** : Allocating cost estimates to individual work items to establish a performance baseline
3. **Controlling costs** : Managing changes to the project budget

#### Basic Principles of Cost Management
- **Life cycle costing** : Consider the total cost of ownership(development + support costs)
- **Cash flow analysis** : Determines estimated annual costs, benefits, and resulting cash flow
- $\text{Profit} = \text{Revenue} - \text{expenses}$
- Early investment in quality can prevent higher costs later (software defect costs increase exponentially when found later)

Cost classifications,
1. Tangible vs Intangible
	- **Tangible costs/benefits** : Costs or benefits that can be easily measured in dollars
	- **Intangible costs/benefits** : Costs or benefits that can be difficult to measure in dollars
2. Direct vs Indirect costs
	- **Direct costs** : Costs that are directly related to producing the products and services of the project.
	- **Indirect costs** : costs that are not directly related to the products or services of the project, but are indirectly related to performing the project.
3. **Sunk cost** : Money that has been spent in the past, when deciding what projects to invest in or continue, 
	Sunk cost should not influence future investment decisions

Additional cost concepts,
1. Learning curve theory 
	- An individual or a company repeats a task or process, they become more efficient and proficient, leading to lower costs and improved output. 
	- When many items are produced repetitively, the unit cost of those items decreases in a regular pattern as more units are produced
2. Reserves
	Money included in a cost estimate to mitigate risk by allowing for future situations that are difficult to predict.
	Two types,
	- **Contingency reserve** : allow for future situations that may be partially planned for. AKA "Known unknowns". Included in the project baseline. 
	- **Management reserves** : allow for future situations that are unpredictable. AKA "Unknown unknowns".

#### Cost management planning
A cost management plan is a document that describes how the organization will manage cost variances on the project.
- Labor often represents a large percentage of total project costs
- Tracking headcount and labor costs is critical
#### Estimating Costs
Types of cost estimations,
1. **Rough Order of Magnitude(ROM)**
	When : Very early in the project life cycle, often 3-5 years before project completion.
	Why : Provide estimates of costs for selection decision
	Accuracy : -25% to +75%
2. **Budgetary**
	When : Early, 1-2 year out
	Why : Puts dollars in the plan
	Accuracy : -10% to +25%
3. Definitive 
	When : Later in the project, less than 1 year out.
	Why : Provide purchases, estimates actual costs
	Accuracy : -5% to +10%
#### Cost estimation techniques
1. **Analogous/top-down estimates**
	Use the actual cost of a previous, similar project as the basis for estimating the cost of the current project.
2. **Bottom-up estimates**
	Involve estimating individual work items or activities and summing them to get a project total
3. **Parametric modeling**
	Uses project characteristics (parameters) in a mathematical model to estimate project costs
4. **Computerized tools**
	Tools, such as spreadsheets and project management software, that can make working with different cost estimates and cost estimation tools easier

#### Constructive Cost Model(COCOMO)
A model for estimating software development costs.
Parameters,
1. Function points : Technology-independent assessments of the functions involved in developing a system
2. Source lines of code(SLOC) : A human-written line of code that is not a blank line or comment
#### Problems with IT Cost estimates
- Complexity of estimating large software projects
- Inexperienced estimators
- Human bias toward underestimation
- Management pressure for lower estimates to win contracts or secure funding
#### Cost estimation best practices
- Understand the purpose of the estimate
- Gather comprehensive project information
- Clarify ground rules and assumptions
- Estimate by major WBS categories when possible
- Create a cost model for easy updating and documentation
