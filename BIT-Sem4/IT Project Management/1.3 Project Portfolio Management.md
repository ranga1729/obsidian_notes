**Project Portfolio Management (PPM)** is the process of selecting, managing, and overseeing a collection of projects within an organization to align with its strategic goals and maximize value. 
Unlike individual project management, which focuses on delivering specific project objectives, PPM ensures that all projects collectively contribute to business priorities, resource optimization, and risk management.

### Project managers vs Program managers
Program : A group of related projects
![[Pasted image 20250325192541.png]]
**Project Managers** : Work with project sponsors, project teams and other people involved in projects to meet project goals. 
**Program Manager** : Oversee programs and often act as bosses for project managers. 

**Project Management Office(PMO)**
An organizational group responsible for coordinating the project management function throughout an organization. 
Possible goals,
- Collect, organize and integrate project data for the entire organization. 
- Develop and maintain templates for project documents.
- Develop and coordinate training for employees.
- Develop and provide formal career path for project managers.
- Provide project management consulting services.
- Provide a structure to project managers.
