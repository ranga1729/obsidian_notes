##### Core Communication Management Processes
1. Communications planning: Determining the information and communications needs of the stakeholders.
2. Information distribution: Making needed information available to project stakeholders in a timely manner.
3. Performance reporting: Collecting and disseminating performance information, including status reports, progress measurement, and forecasting.
4. Managing stakeholders: Managing communications to satisfy the needs and expectations of project stakeholders and to resolve issues

### 1. Communications Planning
Determining the information and communications needs of stakeholders
Communication Management Plan includes,
- Stakeholder communications requirements
- Information format, content, and detail level
- Recipients and producers of information
- Methods/technologies for conveying information
- Communication frequency
- Escalation procedures for issues
- Revision procedures for the plan
- Glossary of terminology

### 2. Information Distribution
Making needed information available to stakeholders in a timely manner
Considerations,
- Using appropriate technology
- Balancing formal and informal methods
- Not burying crucial information
- Not being afraid to report bad news
- Prioritizing face-to-face communication when appropriate
##### Communication Media Effectiveness
Face-to-face communication is,
- 58% of communication through body language
- 35% through how words are said
- 7% through actual content/words

Strategies
- Conduct Short, frequent meetings
- Stand-up meetings over regular meetings
- Limit email use at certain times to encourage direct interaction
- Web conferencing can replace many in-person meetings
### 3. Performance Reporting
Performance reporting keeps stakeholders informed about how resources are being used to achieve project objectives.
Report Types
- Status reports: Project standing at specific points in time
- Progress reports: Team accomplishments during specific periods
- Forecasts: Predictions of future status based on past information/trends
### 4. Managing Stakeholders
- **Definition**: Managing communications to satisfy stakeholder needs and resolve issues

## Improving Project Communications
Suggestions,
1. Manage conflict effectively.
2. Develop better communication skills
3. Run effective meetings. 
4. Use e-mail effectively
5. Use template for project communication
### Conflict Management
Conflict Handling Modes,
    1. Confrontation: Directly use a problem-solving approach
    2. Compromise: Use a Give-and-take approach
    3. Smoothing: De-emphasize areas of difference and emphasize areas of agreement
    4. Forcing: Win-lose approach
    5. Withdrawal: Withdraw from an actual or potential disagreement
- Conflict can produce important results like new ideas, better alternatives, and motivation
- Task-related conflict often improves team performance, while emotional conflict typically reduces it
### Effective Meetings
1. Determine if meeting is necessary
2. Define purpose and intended outcome
3. Identify necessary participants
4. Provide agenda beforehand
5. Prepare materials and logistics in advance
6. Run the meeting professionally
7. Build relationships
### Email Best Practices
1. Ensure email is appropriate for the message
2. Send to the right recipients
3. Use meaningful subject lines
4. Focus on one main subject per email
5. Limit attachments (number and size)
6. Practice good email management (delete unnecessary emails, be cautious with unknown sources)
7. Keep virus protection updated
8. Respond and file emails promptly
9. Learn important email features
### Technology Tools

- Software tools for virtual communications
