- **Project** : a temporary endeavor undertaken to create a unique product, service, or result.
- Every project has a definite beginning and a definite end. 

Advantages of managing a project,
- Better control of finance, physical and human resources.
- Improved customer relations. 
- Shorter development times. 
- Lower costs.
- Higher quality and increased reliability.
- Higher profit margins.
- Improved productivity.
- Better internal coordination.
- Higher worker morale. 

Project attributes,
1. Has a unique purpose.
2. Temporary
3. Developed using progressive elaboration.
4. Requires resources.
5. Should have a primary customer/sponsor.
6. Involves uncertainty.

#### Project vs. Operations
**Project** : A temporary effort undertakes to create a unique product, services or result. It has a defined start and end date, specific objectives, and deliverables.
Ex:
	developing a new website, 
	launching a marketing campaign
**Operation** : Involves ongoing, repetitive activities that sustain an organization's functions. Operations focus on maintaining stability and efficiency and do not have a fixed end date.
Ex: 
	customer support, 
	payroll processing,
	IT system maintenance.

In short, **projects are temporary and unique, while operations are continuous and repetitive**.

What triggers a project ?
1. A market demand
2. A business need
3. A customer request
4. A technological advancement
5. A legal requirement
6. A social need

#### The Triple Constraints
![[Pasted image 20250325173536.png]]
Types of constraints in projects,
1. Scope goals
	The work should be completed.
2. Time goals
	How long it takes to complete.
3. Cost goals
	At what cost it should be completed.

**It's the project manager's duty to balance these three often-competing goals.**
Successful project management means meeting all three goals (scope, time, and cost) – and satisfying the project’s sponsor !
