Main responsibilities, 
- Planning
- Scheduling
- Coordinating
- Working with people to achieve project goals

Job functions,
1. Define scope of project.
2. Identify stakeholders, decision-makers and escalation process.
3. Develop detailed task list. (Work breakdown)
4. Estimate time requirements.
5. Develop initial project management flow chart. 
6. Identify required resources and budget. 
7. Evaluate project requirements.
8. Identify and evaluate risks.
9. Prepare contingency plan.
10. Identify interdependencies.
11. Identify and track critical milestones. 
12. Participate in project phase review. 
13. Secure needed resources.
14. Manage the change control process.
15. Report project status.

Required skills for a Project manager,
1. They should,
	- Be comfortable with changes.
	- Understand the organization they work in and with.
	- Lead team to accomplish project goals. 
2. Hard skills
	Product knowledge
	Using project management tools and techniques.
3. Soft skills
	Being able to work with various type of people.
4. Communication skills
	Listen, persuades
5. Organizational skills.
	plans, sets goals, analyze
6. Team building skills
	Show empath, motivates, promotes the pride and loyalty in the team
7. Leadership skills
	Set examples, Provide vision, being positive and energetic
8. Coping skills
	Flexibility, creativeness, patient, persistent
9. Technology skills
	Experience, Project knowledge


> [!NOTE] Summary of skills
> Be a team player.
> Stay organized and don't be overly emotional.
> Work on projects and for people you believe in.
> Think outside the box.
> Always aiming high.
> Leadership and professionalism.
> Knowing what sponsor expects from the project.
> Learn form own mistakes.
> Trust the team and their decisions.
> Know the business.
> Stand up for yourself.

#### Importance of leadership skills
a leader focuses on long-term goals and big-picture objectives while inspiring people to reach those goals. 
As a manager deal with day-to-day details of meeting specific goals, Project manager often take on both leader and manager roles.
