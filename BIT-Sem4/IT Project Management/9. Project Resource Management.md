#### Introduction
Project Resource Management focuses on making the most effective use of people involved in a project. Effective management of human resources is crucial as people determine the success or failure of organizations and projects.

**Challenges in IT Workforce**:
- Long working hours and constant need to stay updated with technology changes
- Undesirable stereotypes that discourage certain demographics (e.g. women)
- Need for better benefits, work hours, incentives, and human resource management
#### Project Resource Management Processes
1. **Human Resource Planning**: Identifying and documenting project roles, responsibilities, and reporting relationships
2. **Acquiring the Project Team**: Getting needed personnel assigned to the project
3. **Developing the Project Team**: Building individual and group skills to enhance project performance
4. **Managing the Project Team**: Tracking performance, motivating team members, providing feedback, resolving conflicts, and coordinating changes

**Empathic listeners** : People who listen with the intent to understand.

### Acquiring Resources
Recruiting qualified team members is crucial process.
Staffing plans and good hiring procedures are important, as well as incentives for recruiting and retention
Common reasons people leave jobs:
- Not feeling they make a difference
- Do not get proper recognition
- No learning opportunities or personal growth
- Poor relationships with coworkers
- Inadequate compensation

### Resource Loading and Leveling
- **Resource Loading**: Amount of individual resources required during specific periods
- **Overallocation**: When more resources than available are assigned

- **Resource Leveling**: A technique for resolving conflicts by delaying tasks. 
	The main purpose of resource leveling is to create a smoother distribution of resource use and reduce overallocation
	Ex: 
	![[Pasted image 20250517130738.png]]
- **Benefits of Resource Leveling**:
    - More constant resource usage requires less management
    - Enables just-in-time approach for expensive resources
    - Fewer problems for project personnel and accounting
    - Improved morale

### Developing the Project Team
Main goal is to help people work together effectively.
Team Development Stages (Tuckman Model),
![[Pasted image 20250517131234.png]]
Describes the stages that teams typically progress through as they become more cohesive and productive. It outlines four key stages: forming, storming, norming, and performing.
1. **Forming**: Initial stage where team members meet
2. **Storming**: Conflict and disagreement stage
3. **Norming**: Establishing rules and relationships
4. **Performing**: High performance and productivity
5. **Adjourning**: Team disbands after completion

##### Training and Team Building

### Managing the Project Team
Tools and Techniques,
- Observation and conversation
- Project performance appraisals
- Conflict management
- Issue logs
##### General Advice on Teams
- Be patient and kind
- Focus on fixing problems rather than blaming people
- Establish regular, effective meetings
- Allow time for team-building stages
- Limit work teams to 3-7 members
- Plan social activities
- Stress team identity
- Nurture team members and encourage mutual help
- Take additional actions for virtual team members
