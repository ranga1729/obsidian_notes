Projects must operate in broad organizational environment. 
Project manager need to use **System Thinking**.
	System thinking : Taking a holistic view of a project and understanding how it relates to the larger organization.
Senior managers must make sure projects continue to support current business needs. 
### What is a system approach ?
> [!NOTE] System Approach
> Holistic and analytical approach to solving complex problems. 

System approach consist of three part,
1. **System Philosophy**
	View things as a system, which are interacting components that work within an environment to fulfill some purposes.
2. **System Analysis**
	Problem solving approach.
3. **System Management**
	Address business, technological and organizational issues.

### Three-Sphere model for System Management
![[Pasted image 20250326125836.png]]
A framework that divides system management into three key areas.
Each area focuses on the impact of the system due to proposed changes or issues.
1. **Business**
2. **Technology**
3. **Organization**

- Business : Focuses on the impact of proposed changes or issues on the organization's profitability, customer satisfaction, market position and overall business strategy. 
- Technology : Consider the technical aspect of the system including compatibility, cyber security, data privacy, technical feasibility of the proposed changes. 
- Organization : Focuses on the human and organizational aspects such as employee training, communication, resistance to change and the organizational structure and culture.

### Importance of Top Management Commitment

Top management can help project managers,

- Receive cooperation from people throughout the organization.
- Learn how to be better leaders.

#### The need for Organizational Standards
Senior management can encourage,
- The use of standard forms and software for project management. 
- The development and use of guidelines for writing projects plans or providing status information.
- The creation of a project management office or center of excellence. 

#### Project Life Cycles
Project life cycle is a collection of project phases that defines, 
1. What work will be performed in each phase.
2. What deliverables will be produced and when.
3. Who is involved in each phase.
4. How management will control and approve work produced in each phase.

Early phases,
- Low resources needs.
- High uncertainty(risk).
Middle phase,
- Increased certainty of completing a project. 
- More resource needs.
Final phase,
- Focus is ensuring that project requirements were met. 
- Sponsor approves completion of the project.
